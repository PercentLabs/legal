Privacy Policy

Super Simple Roku Controller

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Introduction

Thank you for using Super Simple Roku Controller ("the App"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses and how that information is used.

1. Information Accessed

The App accesses information from Roku devices on the same local network in order to identify and communicate with the Roku device. This may include:

  • The Roku device name.
  • The Roku device's local IP address.
  • The Roku device's unique identifier.

This information is used solely to provide the App's functionality. The App does not collect or store this Roku device information on Percent Labs servers.

2. Internet Access

The App requires an Internet connection to provide certain functionality, including displaying a QR code that directs users to the Super Simple Roku Controller website for device registration and related services.

When you visit our website, information may be processed according to the privacy practices described in the website's applicable privacy policy.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

The Roku App does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect or store personal information on the Roku device or on Percent Labs servers through the App.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not use advertising services or payment processing services within the Roku channel.

5. Data Sharing

Percent Labs does not sell, rent, or share Roku device information accessed by the App with third parties for advertising or other unrelated purposes.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences and is not specifically directed to children. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information processed by the App and our services. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page together with the updated effective date.
